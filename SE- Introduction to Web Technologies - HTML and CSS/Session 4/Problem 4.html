Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Ratings: ★ 8.1/10

Review

A fantastic film that is a roller-coaster of emotions.
So much fun and very grounded.
The emotional beats are brilliant and well-timed.
Definitely a top performance from Tom Holland.
Sadie Sink is the scene stealer of the third act.
Can't go wrong with Spider-Man - just goes to show that when Marvel put in effort, it pays off.
